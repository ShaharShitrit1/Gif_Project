# Academic research group: key findings ([A]=abstract/primary, [S]=secondary, [U]=unverified)
1. Eraker & Ready (JFE 2015) OTCBB/Pink 2000-08: returns extremely negative on avg, highly skewed; formerly-listed >> native OTC [A]. 11,260 firms: avg -40%, MEDIAN -97% per stock [S]; alpha ~ -2%/month [S].
   Ang, Shtauber & Tetlock (RFS 2013), 6,668 firms 1977-2008: OTC market return ~ -0.8%/mo [S]; illiquidity premium "several times" listed; size/value/vol premiums similar; momentum ~3x smaller; premiums biggest for retail-held & non-disclosing; mainly SHORT leg [A/S].
   White (SEC 2016): 1.8M retail trades; median holding-period return -13.4% [S]. Median by tier (non-promoted / promoted): OTCQX +3.0/-20.7; OTCQB -7.9/-14.2; Pink Current -11.0/-14.1; Pink Limited -22.3/-11.2; Grey -45.0/-73.7 [S].
   Bruggemann et al. (RFS 2018): stricter disclosure -> liquidity up, crash risk down [A].
2. Bushee & Leuz (JAE 2005) 1999 OTCBB eligibility: removed firms negative abnormal returns; already-SEC filers positive [A]. Bartlett & Honigsberg (2025 SSRN) 15c2-11: firms becoming compliant got positive returns (+19.5% 3-day subset) [A].
3. Uplisting: Sanger & McConnell 1986 (+ at announcement, - month after listing); Kadlec & McConnell 1994 ~+5% announce; Dharan & Ikenberry 1995 ~-7% 3y post-listing drift in small firms [S]; Davis et al. (Fin Review 2021) $vol +80%, spreads -143bp [S].
   Delisting: Harris, Panchapagesan & Werner 2008: quoted spreads 12.1%->33.9%, eff. spreads 3.3%->9.9%, vol 4.4%->14.3% [A]; delisting-day ~ -19%, post-delisting returns not anomalous [S]. Shumway 1997/1999: missing delisting returns -30% (NYSE/AMEX), -55% (Nasdaq) [A].
4. Going dark: Leuz, Triantis & Wang 2008: ~-10% announcement; 12m long-short vs matched -15.7% [S].
5. Reverse mergers: Adjei, Cyree & Walker 2008: 42% delisted within 3y vs 27% matched IPOs [A]. Floros & Sapp 2011 shells drift down [S].
6. Reverse splits: Desai & Jain 1997: -10.8% 1y, -33.9% 3y abnormal [A]. Hillion & Vermaelen 2004 death-spiral converts: 85% negative 1y returns, ~-34% avg [A/S]. Brophy, Ouimet & Sialm 2009 hedge-fund PIPEs underperform 2y [A/S].
7. Pump & dump: Frieder & Zittrain 2007 spam: buy on touting peak -> -5.5% [A]. Renault 2017: message spike -> price up, sharp reversal in a week [A]. Leuz et al. (Mgmt Sci 2025): 6-8% of active retail investors participate; avg return ~-28 to -30%; median 120-day HPR of pump stocks -67% [S].
8. Positive: Oppenheimer 1986 net-nets 1970-83: 29.4%/yr vs 11.5% [A]. Xiao & Arnold 2008 UK NCAV/MV>1.5: up to 19.7%/yr mkt-adj [A]. Carlisle, Mohanty & Oxman 2010: 2.55%/mo, ~half in January [S]. Mohanty & Oxman 2026 RFE: no abnormal return vs size/industry matched [A]. Arbel et al 1983 neglected firms [A]. Lakonishok & Lee 2001 insider purchases predict returns in small firms [A]. Ikenberry et al 1995 buybacks +12.1% 4y [A].
Traps: survivorship/missing delisting returns; spreads 10-34%; short-side anomalies not harvestable (no borrow); momentum/promotion; microcap anomaly illusion (Hou, Xue & Zhang 2020: 64-65% of anomalies insignificant).
